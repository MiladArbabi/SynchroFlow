## What Is Cin7?

Cin7 is a cloud-based inventory management and order management platform targeting mid-market retailers and wholesalers. It covers multi-channel inventory, purchase order management, warehouse management, manufacturing, B2B portals, and integrations with accounting platforms like Xero and QuickBooks.

It is genuinely powerful. The problem is not capability — it is fit.

## Where Cin7 Breaks Down for Small Shopify Stores

### Implementation Complexity

Cin7 is not a tool you connect and use in an afternoon. A standard implementation involves mapping your product catalogue, configuring warehouse locations, setting up integrations, and in most cases hiring a Cin7 implementation partner. Typical onboarding takes 4 to 12 weeks and costs between £750 and £1,500 before you have processed a single order through the system.

For a merchant with a 3-person warehouse team trying to fix their inventory accuracy problem this week, that timeline is not acceptable.

### Pricing Built for Larger Operations

Cin7 pricing starts at approximately £250 per month for their Core plan — before implementation costs, before add-ons, and before the additional seats you will need as your team grows. The pricing model assumes you have a dedicated operations manager who will get ROI from the full feature set. Most small Shopify merchants are paying for capabilities they will never touch.

### Feature Bloat for Straightforward Warehouse Operations

Cin7 covers manufacturing BOMs, multi-entity accounting consolidation, complex 3PL management, and B2B customer portals. These are genuinely useful capabilities — for the right business. For a Shopify merchant doing £2M in DTC revenue from a single warehouse, they add interface complexity without adding operational value.

## What Small Shopify Merchants Actually Need

The operational problems that drive merchants to evaluate Cin7 are usually one of these:

- Inventory counts in Shopify do not match physical stock
- Pick and pack errors are causing wrong orders to ship
- No visibility into which supplier deliveries are late or short
- Stock-outs are happening because there is no early warning system
- The team is coordinating via WhatsApp and spreadsheets

These are real, serious problems — but they do not require an ERP. They require a warehouse operations layer that is built around Shopify and sized for a small team.
