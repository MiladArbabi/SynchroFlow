## Quick answer

Use **Alerts** to review operational signals that may need attention.

Alerts can point you toward the relevant workflow, such as Warehouse, Orders, Sourcing, Overview, or Problem Center.

## Open Alerts

Open **Alerts** from the app navigation.

The Alerts page has these tabs:

- **Inbox**
- **Snoozed**
- **Resolved**
- **Rules**

This article covers alert review and response. Use **Configure alert rules** for the Rules tab.

## Read the Inbox

The Inbox shows active alerts that are not currently snoozed.

Alerts can include:

- severity;
- source;
- age;
- title;
- message;
- estimated revenue impact where available;
- a link to the related workflow where available.

The page can group alerts by consequence or severity.

Filters can narrow alerts by severity, category, or source.

## Act on an alert

Available actions can include:

- **Acknowledge**: mark the alert as seen while keeping it in the Inbox.
- **Snooze**: park the alert until the selected time.
- **Resolve**: manually close the alert where your role allows it.

Owners and admins can resolve alerts from the current UI.

Operators can acknowledge and snooze alerts they can see.

## Snoozed and resolved alerts

**Snoozed** shows active alerts that are waiting until their snooze time expires.

**Resolved** shows alert history for alerts that have been closed.

Resolved alerts are read-only in the current page.

## Role visibility

Owners and admins can see all alert audiences.

Operators see alerts intended for operators or all users.

## Browser notifications

Browser push notifications may be available when the browser, service worker, notification permission, and LaSyncro notification configuration support them.

Do not use browser notifications as the only source of truth. The Alerts page is the place to review current in-app alerts.

## Alert links

Some alerts include a **Go to** action that opens the related workflow.

If a link does not open the exact page you expected, use the alert text and source to navigate to the closest relevant workflow and continue the review there.
