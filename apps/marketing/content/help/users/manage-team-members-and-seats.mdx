## Quick answer

Owners manage team membership from **Team**.

Admins can view team and member information, but team membership changes are owner-managed.

## Open Team

Open **Settings**, then select **Team**.

The Team page shows active shop members, including:

- name;
- email;
- member since date;
- role;
- team seat usage when the current plan has a finite allowance.

Select a member row to open that member's detail page.

## Understand seat allowances

Each plan includes a team seat allowance:

- **Starter**: 1 seat
- **Core**: 3 seats
- **Growth**: 5 seats
- **Scale**: unlimited seats

LaSyncro can block adding another member once the plan allowance is reached.

Use the seat indicator as a planning signal, then review **Billing** when you need more team capacity.

## Add a team member

Owners can use **Add Member** from the Team page where the current plan allows it.

The add-member form asks for:

- **Email**
- **First Name**
- **Last Name**
- **Role**

Choose **Confirm & Send Invite** to create the member.

When the invite is created, LaSyncro confirms that credentials will be sent by email.

## If the member cannot be added

LaSyncro may stop the add-member flow when:

- the email address is already registered;
- the plan seat allowance has been reached;
- billing setup is needed before extra seats can be added;
- the signed-in user does not have permission to manage team membership.

For Stripe-billed Core and Growth shops, LaSyncro can offer an extra-seat flow when supported by the current subscription.

For Shopify-billed shops, billing changes may route through Shopify billing instead.

## View member details

Member detail pages can show:

- identity information;
- role;
- schedule;
- 30-day performance;
- recent batch activity;
- cost, floor-display, and private notes where those fields are available to the viewer.

Owners manage editable member details and schedules where the current product allows it.
