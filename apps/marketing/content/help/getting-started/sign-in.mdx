## Quick answer

Sign in with the email address and password associated with your LaSyncro account.

If your credentials are accepted, LaSyncro opens the appropriate workspace for your account.

## Sign in

1. Open the LaSyncro sign-in screen.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Enter your password.
4. Select **Sign in**.

If you were previously sent to the sign-in screen from another protected page, LaSyncro may return you to that page after authentication.

Otherwise:

- operators normally continue to the warehouse operations workspace;
- owners and admins normally continue to the Overview.

## If your email or password is incorrect

LaSyncro shows a generic **Invalid email or password** message when the credentials cannot be authenticated.

Check:

- that you entered the correct email address;
- that your password is entered exactly as expected;
- that there are no accidental spaces before or after your password.

For security, LaSyncro does not identify whether the email address or the password was the incorrect value.

## If you forgot your password

Select **Forgot password?** from the sign-in screen.

Follow the password-reset process to request a reset link and choose a new password.
