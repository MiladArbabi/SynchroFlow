## Quick answer

Select **Forgot password?** from the LaSyncro sign-in screen and enter your email address.

If the address belongs to an account, LaSyncro sends a password-reset link. Reset links expire after **30 minutes**.

## Request a password reset

1. Open the LaSyncro sign-in screen.
2. Select **Forgot password?**
3. Enter your email address.
4. Submit the request.
5. Check your inbox for the password-reset email.

LaSyncro shows the same confirmation whether or not the entered email address belongs to an account.

This prevents account information from being exposed through the password-reset screen.

## If you do not receive the email

Check your spam or junk folder.

Make sure you entered the email address associated with your LaSyncro account.

Password-reset requests are limited to **3 requests per 15 minutes per IP address**. If you make too many requests, wait before trying again.

## Use the reset link

Open the password-reset link from the email.

The link is valid for **30 minutes**.

If the link is invalid or has expired, return to **Forgot password?** and request a new one.

## Choose a new password

Your new password must:

- contain between 8 and 72 characters;
- not begin with a space;
- not end with a space.

Enter the new password twice. Both entries must match.

## After the password is reset

When the reset succeeds:

- the reset link can no longer be reused;
- existing active sessions are revoked;
- LaSyncro asks you to sign in again with your new password.

Select **Sign in** and use your new password.
