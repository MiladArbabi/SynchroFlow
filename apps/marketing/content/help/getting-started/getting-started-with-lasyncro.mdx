## Quick answer

Your first warehouse setup links your warehouse to a Shopify Location, maps where stock can physically live, and gets you ready to establish what inventory is physically present.

**Connect Shopify → Choose Shopify Location → Map warehouse basics → Count opening stock**

Start by [connecting your Shopify store](/help/getting-started/connect-your-shopify-store). After **Unlock Insights**, Ops Console introduces the next setup action with **Start here**.

Choose your Shopify Location before mapping your warehouse or counting opening stock. With that Shopify Location mapped, you only need one valid, active **Pick** or **Storage** Stock location to complete **Map warehouse basics** and make **Count opening stock** available. This is the minimum internal mapping milestone, not a fully mapped warehouse or completed inventory reconciliation.

## Start with Ops Console

The **Start here** Spotlight introduces Ops Console as your guide to what needs attention and what to do next. On first entry, it surfaces **Choose Shopify location**, which takes you to **Settings → Warehouse**. Selecting a Shopify Location tells LaSyncro which Shopify inventory location this warehouse represents.

You can close Ops Console and reopen it later to return to your next action.

## Choose your Shopify Location

A **Shopify Location** is Shopify's external inventory location mapped to this LaSyncro warehouse. It is not a shelf, bin, Pick location, Storage location, or another physical place inside your warehouse.

LaSyncro uses your selected Shopify Location for opening-stock expectations and future inventory updates. After you select it in **Settings → Warehouse**, the next setup action is **Map warehouse basics**.

## Define your warehouse floor

With your Shopify Location selected, begin mapping your warehouse floor. The **Set your warehouse boundary** Spotlight introduces the physical floor that will contain your operational locations. In Floor Settings, use **Width** and **Depth** to define that boundary.

**A warehouse boundary is not a Stock location.** It describes the extent of your floor, not a place where inventory is stored or counted. Defining the boundary alone does not complete Map warehouse basics.

## Add your first Stock location

The **Add where you keep your stock** Spotlight guides you to create your first Stock location: a physical place where products can be stored and counted.

Use a code that identifies a real place in your warehouse, such as **PICK-01**, **STORAGE-A-01**, or **BIN-B-04**. Create a valid, active Stock location of type **Pick** or **Storage**.

**You do not need to finish mapping your entire warehouse before continuing.** With your Shopify Location mapped, one valid, active Pick/Storage Stock location satisfies **Map warehouse basics**. You can add more locations as you continue setting up the warehouse.

## Start establishing your inventory truth

Count opening stock requires both a mapped Shopify Location and your first valid, active Pick/Storage Stock location. The setup transition is:

**Shopify Location mapped + first valid, active Pick/Storage Stock location → Map warehouse basics complete → Count opening stock available**

Ops Console can then show **Ready to count opening stock**:

> Your first stock location is mapped. You can start counting now, or keep mapping your warehouse first.

Shopify or system quantities and the stock physically present in your warehouse can differ. Opening count is how you begin establishing what is actually there, in the places where you keep it.

Both choices are valid:

- **Start counting now** if you are ready to establish opening stock.
- **Keep mapping first** if you want to add more of your warehouse locations before counting.

**Opening count does not start automatically.** Making Count opening stock available leaves that choice with you. Your explicit action to start opening count creates the count.

## Common questions

### Do I need to map my entire warehouse first?

No. With your Shopify Location mapped, one valid, active Pick/Storage Stock location completes the minimum mapping milestone. You can start opening count at that point or keep mapping first.

### Why was Count opening stock locked?

Count opening stock requires both a selected Shopify Location and at least one valid, active Pick/Storage Stock location. Setting the warehouse boundary alone does not satisfy those requirements.

### Will LaSyncro start counting automatically?

No. You choose when to start opening count. Completing Map warehouse basics makes it available without starting it.

### Can I add more locations later?

Yes. Your first location is the minimum needed to continue, not the limit of your warehouse map.

### I closed Ops Console. Can I reopen it?

Yes. You can reopen Ops Console later to see what needs attention and return to your next setup action.
