## Quick answer

After connecting Shopify and completing the initial sync, choose which Shopify Location your warehouse represents, map where stock physically lives, and become ready to count what is present.

**Shopify Location → Map warehouse basics → Count opening stock**

Start by [connecting your Shopify store](/help/getting-started/connect-your-shopify-store). After **Unlock Insights**, Ops Console introduces the next setup action with **Start here**.

You only need one valid, active **Pick** or **Storage** Stock location to complete **Map warehouse basics** and make **Count opening stock** available. This is the minimum mapping milestone, not a fully mapped warehouse or completed inventory reconciliation.

## Start with Ops Console

The **Start here** Spotlight introduces Ops Console as your guide to what needs attention and what to do next. On first entry, start with **Choose Shopify location** if your warehouse is not yet linked to one.

In **Settings → Warehouse**, choose the Shopify Location this warehouse represents. Connecting your store is a separate earlier step; it does not replace this warehouse mapping. Once the Shopify Location is chosen, continue with **Map warehouse basics**, then **Count opening stock**.

Fixing product costs, printing location barcodes, connecting a shipping carrier, releasing a batch, and inviting pickers are not mandatory milestones in this first-entry journey. Use those capabilities in their relevant workflows when needed.

You can close Ops Console and reopen it later to return to your next action.

## Define your warehouse floor

The **Set your warehouse boundary** Spotlight introduces the physical floor that will contain your operational locations.

1. Open **Floor settings**.
2. Enter **Width** and **Depth**.
3. Click **Save & continue** to confirm the boundary.
4. Continue to **Add where you keep your stock**.

Both dimensions must be valid and positive, and the boundary must include any existing positioned floor components before **Save & continue** is available. Width and Depth remain drafts until you confirm them; opening Floor settings, typing, leaving a field, or pressing Enter does not save the boundary or advance onboarding.

**A warehouse boundary is not a Stock location.** It describes the extent of your floor, not a place where inventory is stored or counted. Confirming the boundary does not complete **Map warehouse basics**. **Count opening stock** remains unavailable until one valid, active **Pick** or **Storage** Stock location exists.

## Add your first Stock location

The **Add where you keep your stock** Spotlight guides you to create your first Stock location: a physical place where products can be stored and counted.

Use a code that identifies a real place in your warehouse, such as **PICK-01**, **STORAGE-A-01**, or **BIN-B-04**. Create a valid, active Stock location of type **Pick** or **Storage**.

**You do not need to finish mapping your entire warehouse before continuing.** One valid Pick/Storage Stock location satisfies **Map warehouse basics**. You can add more locations as you continue setting up the warehouse.

## Start establishing your inventory truth

With the warehouse’s Shopify Location chosen and your first valid Stock location created, the setup transition is:

**First valid Stock location → Map warehouse basics complete → Count opening stock available**

Ops Console can then show **Ready to count opening stock**:

> Your first stock location is mapped. You can start counting now, or keep mapping your warehouse first.

Shopify or system quantities and the stock physically present in your warehouse can differ. Opening count is how you begin establishing what is actually there, in the places where you keep it.

Both choices are valid:

- **Start counting now** if you are ready to establish opening stock.
- **Keep mapping first** if you want to add more of your warehouse locations before counting.

**Opening count does not start automatically.** Making Count opening stock available leaves that choice with you.

## Common questions

### Do I need to map my entire warehouse first?

No. After choosing the warehouse’s Shopify Location, one valid, active Pick/Storage Stock location completes the minimum mapping milestone. You can start opening count at that point or keep mapping first.

### Why was Count opening stock locked?

Choose the warehouse’s Shopify Location first, then create one valid, active Pick/Storage Stock location. These prerequisites make Count opening stock available. Setting the warehouse boundary alone does not satisfy them.

### Will LaSyncro start counting automatically?

No. You choose when to start opening count. Completing Map warehouse basics makes it available without starting it.

### Can I add more locations later?

Yes. Your first location is the minimum needed to continue, not the limit of your warehouse map.

### I closed Ops Console. Can I reopen it?

Yes. You can reopen Ops Console later to see what needs attention and return to your next setup action.
