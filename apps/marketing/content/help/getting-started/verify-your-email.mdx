## Quick answer

After creating your account, open the verification email from LaSyncro and follow its verification link.

Each verification link is valid for **30 minutes**.

After successful verification, LaSyncro continues your onboarding to the **Connect store** step.

## Verify your email

1. Open the inbox for the email address you used to register.
2. Find the LaSyncro verification email.
3. Open the verification link.
4. Wait for LaSyncro to confirm that your email has been verified.
5. Continue to the Connect store step.

## If you cannot find the email

Check your spam or junk folder first.

The Check your inbox screen also provides a direct mailbox button for supported email providers such as Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo Mail, and iCloud Mail.

If you are still signed in, select **Resend email** to request another verification message.

## If the verification link expired

Verification links expire after 30 minutes.

Return to LaSyncro and request another verification email. The new email contains a fresh verification link with a new 30-minute validity period.

Verification emails can be resent at most once every 60 seconds.

## If you are no longer signed in

Sign in again to request another verification email.

If you registered with the wrong email address, return to registration and use the correct address.

## If verification still fails

If a fresh verification link is still rejected, contact **contact@lasyncro.com** and include the email address you are trying to verify and the error shown by LaSyncro.
