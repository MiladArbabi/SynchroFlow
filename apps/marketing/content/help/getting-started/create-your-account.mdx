## Quick answer

Create your account with your name, email address, and password, then accept the Terms and Privacy policy.

After registration succeeds, LaSyncro signs you in and takes you to the email-verification step.

## What you need

Enter:

- your first name;
- your last name;
- a valid email address;
- a password;
- acceptance of the Terms and Privacy policy.

Your first and last names must each be between 2 and 50 characters.

Names can contain letters, spaces, apostrophes, and hyphens.

## Password requirements

Your password must:

- contain between 8 and 72 characters;
- not begin with a space;
- not end with a space.

## Create your account

1. Open the LaSyncro registration screen.
2. Enter your first name and last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Create a password that meets the requirements above.
5. Accept the Terms and Privacy policy.
6. Submit the registration form.

When registration succeeds, your account is created and LaSyncro takes you to **Check your inbox**.

## What happens next

LaSyncro sends a verification email to the address you registered with.

You must verify that email address before continuing through onboarding.

If you do not see the email, continue with **Verify your email address** for resend and troubleshooting steps.
