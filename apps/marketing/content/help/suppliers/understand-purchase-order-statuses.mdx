## Quick answer

Purchase-order statuses show where inbound stock is in the supplier-to-warehouse journey.

The standard lifecycle is:

**Draft → Ordered → Confirmed → In production → Shipped → Partially received → Received**

A purchase order can also be **Cancelled**.

Receiving begins from the **Shipped** stage.

## Draft

A newly created purchase order starts as **Draft**.

At this stage, the PO has been created but has not yet progressed into the supplier fulfillment process.

Review the supplier, Shopify-linked products, quantities, and other PO information before advancing it.

## Ordered

**Ordered** indicates that the purchase order has moved beyond draft and the stock has been ordered from the supplier.

## Confirmed

**Confirmed** indicates that the supplier order has progressed to the confirmed stage.

## In production

**In production** represents stock that is still being prepared before shipment.

The LaSyncro interface may present this stage as **On the way** in parts of the supplier workflow.

## Shipped

**Shipped** is the key handoff into receiving.

Once the PO is shipped, you can explicitly start a receive job for that purchase order.

Changing the PO to Shipped does not itself receive the stock or add inventory.

The receive job must still be started and processed.

## Partially received

A PO becomes **Partially received** when some ordered stock has been accepted through receiving but the entire purchase order has not yet been received.

For example, this can occur with a partial delivery.

LaSyncro supports multiple receive events for the same PO when stock arrives in separate deliveries.

## Received

A PO becomes **Received** when the ordered quantities have been fully received.

Accepted quantities recorded during receiving determine the received progress of each PO line.

## Cancelled

**Cancelled** is used when the purchase order is no longer continuing through the inbound workflow.

Cancelled purchase orders do not proceed to normal receiving.

## When can receiving begin?

A PO must reach **Shipped** before its WMS receive job is created.

Starting receiving is an explicit action.

The sequence is:

**Shipped PO → Start receive job → Inspect or scan stock → Close receiving → Create stow tasks**

This preserves the distinction between stock expected from a supplier and stock that has physically arrived and been accepted into the warehouse.

## Who manages PO statuses

Purchase-order status management is intended for owners and admins.

Operators work with the downstream warehouse tasks rather than managing the purchase-order lifecycle.
