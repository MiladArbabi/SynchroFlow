## Quick answer

A stow task does not have to be completed in one placement.

LaSyncro records how many units have actually been placed and keeps the task active until its full quantity is complete.

If a unit is missing or damaged, report a stow exception instead of recording stock as successfully placed.

## Partial stowing

Each successful physical scan places one unit.

LaSyncro accumulates those placements in the task's progress.

For example, if a task contains 5 units and 3 have been successfully placed:

- total quantity: 5;
- placed: 3;
- remaining: 2.

The task remains active for the remaining units.

It does not become complete until all 5 units have been placed.

## Report a stow exception

The stow workflow supports these exception types:

- **Item missing**
- **Product defect**
- **Packaging defect**

Use an exception when the physical stock cannot be stowed normally.

Owners, admins, and operators can report WMS exceptions.

## What happens after an exception is reported

Reporting a stow exception creates an open task in the **Problem Center**.

LaSyncro records the affected quantity and routes the issue for follow-up.

The exception report itself does **not** immediately reduce or otherwise change inventory.

Inventory impact is deferred until the Problem Center issue is reviewed and resolved.

This keeps the physical inventory decision separate from the operator's initial problem report.

## Problem bin requirement

The warehouse must have a Problem bin configured before a stow exception can be reported successfully.

The Problem Center task is associated with that configured problem location for follow-up.

## Do not complete inventory that was not placed

Do not record a missing or defective unit as successfully stowed just to finish the task.

Only physical units that were successfully placed in a confirmed storage location should count toward stow progress.

## Continue with the remaining stock

Valid units can continue through the normal stow process.

The remaining task quantity stays available until its required placements are completed or the associated problem is handled through the appropriate exception workflow.

This preserves the distinction between:

**stock physically stowed successfully** and **stock requiring operational resolution**.
