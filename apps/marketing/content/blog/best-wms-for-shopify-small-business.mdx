Most small Shopify merchants discover they need a WMS the hard way — after an oversold order, a customer complaint about a stockout that Shopify didn't flag, or a stock count that reveals their Shopify numbers have drifted significantly from physical reality.

This guide cuts through the noise. Here is what actually matters when choosing a WMS for a small Shopify operation, what each tool is genuinely built for, and how to match your situation to the right choice.

## What "Small Business" Actually Means for WMS Selection

Before comparing tools, be precise about your situation. The WMS market segments sharply by scale:

- **Under 50 orders/day, 1-2 warehouse staff:** Shopify native tools are probably sufficient. A WMS adds cost and process overhead that may not be justified yet.
- **50-200 orders/day, 2-10 warehouse staff:** This is the sweet spot where a purpose-built SMB WMS pays for itself within weeks. Pick errors, inventory drift, and manual reconciliation are costing you more than any tool subscription.
- **200-1,000 orders/day, 10-30 warehouse staff:** You need a WMS with workforce coordination, shift management, and robust reporting. Still SMB territory — but the wrong tool here causes real operational damage.
- **1,000+ orders/day or multichannel:** Enterprise tools become relevant. The implementation cost and complexity is justified by the operational complexity.

<NumberedH3 n="2" title="Cin7 — mid-market operations with manufacturing and B2B requirements" />

Cin7 is a powerful inventory and operations platform built for mid-market businesses with dedicated operations staff, IT support, and complex requirements — manufacturing BOMs, multi-entity accounting, B2B customer portals, and advanced 3PL management.

**Who it is for:** Businesses doing $5M-$50M in revenue with dedicated operations teams, manufacturing or assembly requirements, or complex B2B wholesale operations alongside their Shopify DTC channel.

**What it does not cover:** Simple Shopify warehouse operations without the enterprise feature set. Most small Shopify merchants are paying for 80% of Cin7's functionality they will never use.

**Pricing:** Approximately $250/month starting price. $750-$1,500 implementation cost. 4-12 week onboarding timeline.

<NumberedH3 n="3" title="Linnworks — high-volume multichannel retailers" />

Linnworks is built for high-volume retailers selling across dozens of channels — Shopify, Amazon, eBay, Walmart, freight carriers — with dedicated operations teams managing thousands of orders per day.

**Who it is for:** Retailers processing 1,000+ orders per day across multiple marketplaces who need centralised listing management, shipping automation, and multichannel inventory sync.

**What it does not cover:** Simple Shopify-only warehouse operations. For a DTC Shopify brand doing 200 orders per day from one warehouse, Linnworks is dramatically overbuilt and overpriced.

**Pricing:** Quote-only, typically starting at $449/month. Significant implementation and onboarding costs.

<NumberedH3 n="4" title="Brightpearl — omnichannel retail with ERP requirements" />

Brightpearl combines retail ERP, warehouse management, and multichannel order management for established omnichannel retailers with physical stores, wholesale, and online channels operating simultaneously.

**Who it is for:** Established retailers with $10M+ in revenue, multiple physical locations, wholesale and retail channels, and the need for a full retail ERP system rather than a standalone WMS.

**Pricing:** Enterprise pricing, typically $375-$1,500/month depending on order volume and modules required.

<NumberedH3 n="5" title="Shopify native inventory — for early-stage operations" />

Shopify's built-in inventory tracking records stock levels per variant, supports multiple locations, and handles basic transfer records. It is free, always connected, and requires no setup beyond enabling inventory tracking on your products.

**Who it is for:** Merchants doing under 50 orders per day with a single warehouse operator and manageable SKU counts. The moment you have multiple operators, meaningful SKU complexity, or more than 50 daily orders, native Shopify inventory accumulates drift faster than manual reconciliation can address.

**What it does not cover:** Pick and pack workflows, bin-level location tracking, scan-based receiving, supplier performance management, or any of the operational features that define a true WMS.

## How to Choose

If your situation is:

- **Shopify-only, 1-30 staff, 50-1,000 orders/day, own warehouse** → LaSyncro
- **High-volume multichannel across Amazon/eBay/Walmart** → Linnworks or Veeqo
- **Manufacturing, assembly, or complex B2B wholesale** → Cin7
- **Omnichannel retail with physical stores and ERP needs** → Brightpearl
- **Under 50 orders/day, single operator** → Shopify native (for now)

The most common mistake small Shopify merchants make is choosing a tool based on brand recognition — Cin7 and Linnworks have large marketing budgets — rather than operational fit. Both are genuinely excellent tools for the operations they are built for. Neither is built for a 10-person Shopify warehouse team doing 200 orders a day.

## The Signal That You Have Outgrown Shopify Native

You do not need a WMS on day one. You need one when:

1. You spend more than 30 minutes per day reconciling inventory discrepancies manually
2. You discover stockouts from customer complaints rather than your own system
3. More than one person works in the warehouse with no coordinated pick process
4. Your last stock count revealed a gap of more than 2% between Shopify's records and physical stock
5. You have had at least one oversold order in the last 90 days

If three or more of these are true, every week without a WMS is costing you more than the tool subscription.
