# ActivationSurface Doctrine

**Status:** LOCKED
**Applies to:** All LaSyncro modules (current and future)
**Audience:** Product, Engineering, Design, Growth
**Version:** v1.0

---

## 0. Purpose (Non-Negotiable)

The **ActivationSurface** exists to eliminate **decision uncertainty** at the moment of integration.

It is **not**:

* An onboarding page
* A marketing surface
* A feature explanation
* A tutorial

It **is**:

> A decision-forcing surface that makes the cost of inaction explicit and unacceptable.

If a merchant reaches an ActivationSurface and leaves without activating, the surface has failed.

---

## 1. Core Principle: Decision Certainty

**Activation happens when this equation is true in the merchant’s mind:**

```
Cost of current ignorance > Effort to activate
```

The ActivationSurface must **force this realization**, not persuade or encourage it.

---

## 2. The Irreversible Truth (Mandatory)

Every ActivationSurface **must explicitly state** the following idea (wording may vary, meaning must not):

> **“Decisions are already being made. Activation only determines whether they’re informed.”**

This sentence exists to eliminate the false belief that “doing nothing” is neutral.

Without this, “later” remains a valid option — and conversion drops.

---

## 3. ActivationSurface Laws (Hard Rules)

These are **laws**, not guidelines.
Violating any law invalidates the ActivationSurface.

---

### LAW 1 — Ignorance Must Be Visible

❌ Forbidden:

* “You don’t have visibility”
* “Insights are unavailable”
* “Data is missing”

✅ Required:

* Show **specific unknowns**
* Show **blocked decisions**
* Show **unavailable answers**

Ignorance must be **seen**, not described.

---

### LAW 2 — One Decision Only

Each ActivationSurface may block **exactly one decision**.

| Module              | Blocked Decision                        |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| OrderNexus          | Which orders lost money                 |
| SKU-OS              | Which SKUs will stock out first         |
| Specter (Customers) | Which customers cost money              |
| InsightCore         | What actually caused performance change |
| MarginCore          | Which cost assumptions are wrong        |

If more than one decision appears, the surface fails.

---

### LAW 3 — Absence > Promise

❌ Showing benefits
❌ Explaining value
❌ Listing features

✅ Showing **locked insight placeholders**
✅ Showing **unknown states**
✅ Showing **redacted truth**

The merchant must *feel* what they cannot currently know.

---

### LAW 4 — Activation Is the Only Way Forward

An ActivationSurface must **remove exploration paths**.

❌ Navigation
❌ Secondary actions
❌ “Learn more” detours

The CTA must be the **only forward motion**.

---

### LAW 5 — Trust Copy Placement Is Fixed

Trust & reassurance content must appear:

> **Immediately below the primary CTA**

Anywhere else reduces its effectiveness.

Trust is not persuasion — it is hesitation interception.

---

### LAW 6 — Time Is Never the Primary Motivator

❌ “Takes 30 seconds”
❌ “Quick setup”
❌ “Fast integration”

These may appear **only after inevitability is established**.

Primary motivation must always be:

> Continuing to operate blind causes damage.

---

### LAW 7 — Activation Is Hygiene, Not Growth

Never frame activation as:

* Improvement
* Optimization
* Growth
* Upside

Always frame it as:

> **Baseline operational hygiene**

Growth language invites procrastination.
Hygiene language eliminates it.

---

## 4. Canonical ActivationSurface Structure (Locked)

Every ActivationSurface **must follow this order**.

No reordering. No omissions.

---

### 1️⃣ Identity + Blind Spot (One Sentence)

State what is currently **indistinguishable**.

**Example:**

> “Right now, profitable and unprofitable orders are indistinguishable.”

---

### 2️⃣ Ignorance Visualization

Show a **non-interactive** example of missing truth.

**Example:**

```
Order #4832
Margin: ⚫ Unknown
Reason:
– Shipping cost missing
– Payment fees unaccounted
– True product cost unavailable
```

---

### 3️⃣ Absence Statement

Make the ignorance explicit.

**Example:**

> “This order could be losing money. You wouldn’t know.”

---

### 4️⃣ Irreversible Truth

State the non-neutrality of inaction.

**Mandatory concept:**

> Decisions are already being made.

---

### 5️⃣ Post-Activation Certainty (Single Outcome)

State **one concrete result** — no lists.

**Example:**

> “Once connected, money-losing orders are identified automatically.”

---

### 6️⃣ Primary CTA

One action. One verb.

**Example:**

> **Connect Shopify Store**

---

### 7️⃣ Trust Intercept (Immediately Below CTA)

Short, factual, non-marketing.

**Example:**

> Read-only access • No store changes • Disconnect anytime
> No PII stored • Encrypted • Verified permissions

---

### 8️⃣ Commitment Gradient (Optional)

Only allowed if it **reduces uncertainty**.

Allowed steps:

* Preview locked insights
* Confirm permissions
* Begin connection

Forbidden:

* Exploration
* Evaluation language
* “See what’s possible”

---

### 9️⃣ Post-Click Expectation (Deterministic)

Tie expectations to **system behavior**, not promises.

**Example:**

> Orders sync → margins compute → insights appear

---

## 5. Conversion Philosophy (Locked)

* Truth accelerates trust
* Trust accelerates adoption
* Certainty eliminates hesitation

The ActivationSurface does **not** convince.
It **removes tolerance for ignorance**.

---

## 6. What Is Explicitly Forbidden

* Fake urgency (countdowns, scarcity)
* Exaggerated capabilities
* Hidden requirements
* Social proof as primary motivator
* Competitive comparisons
* Misleading simplicity
* Fear-based threats

---

## 7. Success Criteria (Acceptance Checklist)

An ActivationSurface may ship **only if all are true**:

1. One dominant decision is blocked
2. Ignorance is visually demonstrated
3. Absence is shown, not promised
4. Cost of inaction is explicit
5. CTA is the only forward path
6. Trust copy is placed under CTA
7. No scroll required for core message
8. Post-activation outcome is deterministic
9. No growth or marketing language
10. No alternative actions presented

---

## 8. Strategic Outcome

When implemented correctly:

The merchant does not think:

> “Should I activate?”

They think:

> **“Why am I tolerating this?”**

At that point, activation is not a decision —
it is **operational hygiene**.

---

**End of Doctrine**
Any deviation requires a versioned update and explicit approval.
