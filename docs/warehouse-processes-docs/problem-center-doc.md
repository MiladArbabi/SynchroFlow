# **Problem-Center (Handling Issues During Picking & Packing)**

---

## 1. What Is Problem-Center?

Problem-Center is:

→ **how LaSyncro handles issues that occur during active work**

---

These are not inventory problems (SKU-Gaps).

These are:
→ **workflow interruptions**

---

Examples:

* item not found
* item damaged during picking
* wrong item in location
* barcode not scannable
* missing item in order

---

## 2. The Real Problem (Without a System)

In most warehouses:

When something goes wrong:

* work stops
* people ask around
* decisions are made on the spot

---

This leads to:

* delays
* inconsistent decisions
* untracked issues

---

And worst of all:

→ **no record of what actually happened**

---

## 3. What LaSyncro Does Instead

When a problem occurs:

→ it becomes part of the workflow

---

Instead of:

* stopping
* guessing
* bypassing

---

LaSyncro:

* captures the issue
* logs it
* routes it

---

Work continues.

---

## 4. When Problem-Center Is Used

Problem-Center is triggered during:

---

### Picking

* item not in location
* item damaged
* wrong item found

---

### Packing

* missing item
* wrong item detected
* product issue discovered

---

## Key Insight

→ these problems happen **during execution**

Not before. Not after.

---

## 5. What Happens When a Problem Is Detected

---

### Step 1 — Capture the Issue

The operator:

* flags the issue in the app
* selects the type (missing, damaged, wrong, etc.)

---

### Step 2 — The System Logs It

Automatically recorded:

* order
* SKU
* location
* time
* operator

---

### Step 3 — The Workflow Continues

The system may:

* skip the item
* move to next task
* reroute the order

---

No blocking. No confusion.

---

### Step 4 — The Issue Is Assigned

→ someone is responsible for resolving it

---
