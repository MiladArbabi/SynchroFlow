# **What Are SKU-Gaps (And Why They Matter)**

---

## 1. What Is a SKU-Gap?

A SKU-Gap is:

→ **anything wrong with an item in your inventory**

---

This includes:

* damaged products
* wrong items received
* packaging defects
* unreadable or missing barcodes
* incorrect quantities

---

These are not rare.

They happen every day.

---

## 2. The Real Problem (Most Warehouses Ignore This)

In most warehouses:

* problems are noticed
* worked around
* or ignored

---

Examples:

* “Just use another one”
* “We’ll fix it later”
* “It’s probably fine”

---

What actually happens:

→ bad inventory stays in the system
→ problems resurface during picking
→ errors reach customers

---

SKU-Gaps are not the issue.

→ **Ignoring them is.**

---

## 3. What LaSyncro Does Differently

LaSyncro treats every SKU-Gap as:

→ **a tracked, actionable task**

---

The moment a problem is detected:

* it is recorded
* it is categorized
* it becomes a **Job To Be Done**

---

Nothing disappears.

Nothing is assumed.

---

## 4. Where SKU-Gaps Are Detected

SKU-Gaps can appear at any step:

---

### During Receiving

* wrong item delivered
* damaged product
* quantity mismatch

---

### During Stowing

* item doesn’t match expected
* labeling issues

---

### During Picking

* item found damaged
* wrong item in location
* barcode unreadable

---

### During Packing

* item mismatch
* product defect noticed

---

## Key Insight

→ SKU-Gaps are not tied to a step
→ they are tied to the **item itself**

---

## 5. What Happens When a SKU-Gap Is Created

When a problem is identified:

---

### 1. It Is Logged

* linked to the SKU
* linked to the location
* linked to the moment it was found

---

### 2. It Becomes a Job To Be Done

Examples:

* inspect item
* relabel barcode
* replace product
* remove from inventory

---

### 3. It Is Assigned

→ someone is responsible for fixing it

---

### 4. It Stays Visible Until Resolved

No forgetting.
No “we’ll get to it later.”

---

## 6. Why This Matters

If SKU-Gaps are not managed:

* picking errors increase
* packing errors increase
* returns increase
* refunds increase

---

If SKU-Gaps are managed:

→ problems are stopped early

---

This means:

* cleaner inventory
* faster operations
* fewer downstream issues

---

## 7. What Good Looks Like

* issues are captured immediately
* nothing is ignored
* every problem has an owner
* resolution is part of the workflow

---

## 8. Common Mistakes to Avoid

* ignoring small defects
* continuing workflow without logging issues
* fixing problems without recording them
* assuming someone else will handle it

---

If it’s not logged:

→ it doesn’t exist in the system
→ and it will come back later

---

## 9. How SKU-Gaps Connect to Everything Else

SKU-Gaps directly impact:

* picking accuracy
* packing verification
* returns
* refunds

---

They are the root cause of many issues.

---

Managing them properly means:

→ fewer problems everywhere else
