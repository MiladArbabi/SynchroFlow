# **Stowing Inventory (Assigning Locations Correctly)**

---

## 1. What Stowing Means in LaSyncro

Stowing is not just putting items on a shelf.

It is:
→ **assigning a permanent, known location to every item**

---

Without LaSyncro:

* items get placed “where there’s space”
* locations are remembered (or forgotten)
* finding items becomes slow and inconsistent

---

With LaSyncro:

→ Every item has:

* a defined location
* a recorded placement
* a known position for future picking

---

## 2. What You Need Before You Start

* Items already **received and confirmed**
* Shelving or storage locations set up
* LaSyncro app open on your phone

---

## 3. The Stowing Flow (Simple)

```text id="c8u2nx"
Assign → Place → Confirm
```

No guessing. No memory.

---

## 4. Step-by-Step

---

### Step 1 — Open Stowing on Your Phone

* Open LaSyncro
* Go to the stowing task

You will see:
→ items ready to be placed

---

### Step 2 — Scan the Item (or Select It)

* scan the item barcode
  OR
* select from the list

---

### What happens:

→ the system prepares to assign a location

---

### Step 3 — Follow the Suggested Location

LaSyncro will:
→ indicate **where the item should go**

This may be:

* a shelf
* a bin
* a specific slot

---

### Step 4 — Place the Item

* move to the location
* place the item exactly where indicated

---

### Step 5 — Confirm Placement

* scan the location (if applicable)
  OR
* confirm on your phone

---

Now:

→ the item is **officially stored and tracked**

---

## 5. What Changes Operationally

Before:

* “Where did we put this?”
* “Check that shelf… maybe the other one…”

---

After:

→ every item has:

* a location
* a record
* a clear retrieval path

---

## 6. Why This Step Is Critical

If stowing is inconsistent:

* picking slows down
* items get “lost”
* team size increases unnecessarily

---

If stowing is correct:

→ everything downstream becomes faster and easier

---

## 7. What Good Stowing Looks Like

* items are placed once
* location is recorded instantly
* no duplicate placements
* no searching later

---

## 8. Common Mistakes to Avoid

* placing items “near” the location instead of exact
* skipping confirmation
* creating unofficial overflow spots

---

If you break location discipline:
→ the system loses reliability

---

## 9. How This Connects to Picking

Picking depends entirely on stowing.

If this step is correct:

→ picking becomes:

* fast
* precise
* effortless

---

If not:

→ picking becomes:

* slow
* error-prone
* frustrating
