# **Receiving Inventory (Scan, Verify, Log)**

---

## 1. What Receiving Means in LaSyncro

Receiving is not just unloading boxes.

It is:
→ **confirming exactly what entered your system**

---

Without LaSyncro:

* items arrive
* someone checks a list
* mistakes slip in unnoticed

---

With LaSyncro:

→ Every item is:

* scanned
* verified
* recorded instantly

---

## 2. What You Need Before You Start

* Products (SKUs) already added in LaSyncro
* A smartphone with the app installed
* Incoming inventory ready to be received

---

## 3. The Receiving Flow (Simple)

```text
Scan → Confirm → Move
```

That’s it.

No paperwork. No double entry.

---

## 4. Step-by-Step

---

### Step 1 — Open Receiving on Your Phone

* Open the LaSyncro app
* Go to the receiving task

You will see:
→ what is expected (if applicable)
→ or a ready state to scan items

---

### Step 2 — Scan the Item

* Use your phone camera
* scan the barcode on the product

---

### What happens instantly:

* the item is identified
* the system checks if it matches expectations

---

### Step 3 — Confirm the Item

You will see one of three outcomes:

---

#### ✅ Correct Item

→ item matches
→ automatically logged

---

#### ⚠️ Issue Detected (SKU-Gap)

Examples:

* damaged item
* wrong product
* barcode issue

---

When this happens:

→ the issue is captured
→ becomes a **Job To Be Done**
→ does NOT get ignored

---

#### ❓ Unknown Item

* not recognized in system

→ you decide:

* add it
* flag it

---

### Step 4 — Move the Item Forward

Once confirmed:

→ item is ready for **stowing**

No need to:

* write anything down
* update spreadsheets
* inform anyone

---

## 5. What Changes Operationally

Before:

* “Did we receive this?”
* “Was it correct?”
* “Who checked it?”

---

After:

→ every item has:

* a record
* a timestamp
* a status

---

## 6. How Problems Are Handled (Important)

Receiving is where most problems enter your system.

LaSyncro ensures they are:

* detected immediately
* recorded properly
* assigned for resolution

---

This prevents:

* bad inventory entering shelves
* future picking errors
* returns caused by upstream mistakes

---

## 7. What Good Receiving Looks Like

* items are scanned once
* issues are captured immediately
* no manual tracking
* no follow-up confusion

---

## 8. Common Mistakes to Avoid

* scanning but not confirming issues
* ignoring damaged items
* trying to “fix later”

---

If it’s wrong at receiving:
→ it becomes a bigger problem later

---

## 9. Where to Go Next

After receiving:

→ **Stowing Inventory (Assigning Locations Correctly)**

This is where items get a permanent place.
