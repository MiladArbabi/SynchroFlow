## 3. The System Behind the Workflow

What makes LaSyncro different is not the steps.

It’s what happens **during and around them**.

---

### A. problem-center (Inventory Problems)

Sometimes inventory isn’t right:

* damaged items
* wrong products
* packaging issues
* unreadable barcodes

Most warehouses:
→ ignore or work around these

---

**LaSyncro does something different:**

→ These issues are captured immediately
→ Turned into **clear tasks (Jobs To Be Done)**
→ Managed until resolved

---

### B. Problem-Center (Operational Interruptions)

During picking or packing, things break:

* item not found
* item damaged
* wrong item discovered
* barcode won’t scan

---

Instead of:
→ stopping work
→ asking around
→ guessing

---

**LaSyncro:**

→ captures the problem
→ logs it
→ routes it properly

So work continues without confusion.

---

### C. Returns & Refunds (Feedback Loop)

Orders don’t always end at shipping.

They come back.

---

When they do, LaSyncro helps you:

* inspect returned items

* decide:

  * restock
  * fix
  * reject

* connect the return to:

  * the original order
  * the root cause

---

This turns returns into:
→ **insight, not loss**
