# **Barcode Not Scanning**

---

## 1. What You’re Seeing

You try to scan an item.

The barcode:

* does not scan
* is not recognized
* or takes multiple attempts

---

## 2. What This Actually Means

This is not just a scanning issue.

It usually means:

* barcode is damaged or worn
* barcode is missing
* barcode is incorrect for the item
* lighting or angle is affecting scan

---

## Key Insight

→ If it cannot be scanned
→ it cannot be reliably tracked

---

## 3. What To Do (Immediately)

Follow this exactly:

---

### Step 1 — Try a Clean Scan

* hold the phone steady
* adjust angle
* improve lighting
* ensure barcode is fully visible

---

### Step 2 — Try Again (Quick Only)

* retry 1–2 times max

👉 do NOT waste time repeatedly scanning

---

### Step 3 — If Still Not Scanning

→ **Log the issue in Problem-Center**

* select: **“Barcode Not Scannable”**
* confirm

---

### Step 4 — Continue the Workflow

* move to the next item
* do NOT stop the process

---

## 4. What NOT To Do

Do NOT:

* manually assume the item is correct
* type in item details (if avoidable)
* bypass scanning
* delay the workflow

---

If you skip scanning:

→ the system loses accuracy
→ errors become untraceable

---

## 5. What Happens Next (System Behavior)

Once logged:

* issue is recorded
* linked to:

  * SKU
  * location
  * order

---

Then:

→ becomes a **SKU-Gap (Job To Be Done)**

---

This ensures:

* barcode is fixed or replaced
* item becomes usable again
* future scans work correctly

---

## 6. Why This Matters

Barcodes are how the system:

* identifies items
* confirms correctness
* tracks movement

---

If scanning fails and is ignored:

→ mistakes increase
→ tracking breaks
→ trust drops

---

## 7. Root Cause (Why This Happens)

Common causes:

* damaged or scratched labels
* poor printing quality
* incorrect barcode assignment
* wear over time

---

## 8. What Good Looks Like

* every item scans cleanly
* issues are logged immediately
* no manual overrides
* system remains accurate

---

## 9. How To Prevent This

* ensure high-quality barcode labels
* replace damaged labels quickly
* avoid handling that damages labels
* log barcode issues immediately

---
