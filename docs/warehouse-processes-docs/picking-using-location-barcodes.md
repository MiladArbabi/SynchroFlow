# **Picking Using Location Barcodes**

---

## 1. What This Means

Picking is no longer:

→ “search for the item”

It is:

→ **go to the exact location → confirm → pick**

---

You are not navigating the warehouse.

→ **you are following instructions**

---

## 2. The Rule

→ **Always verify both: location AND item**

---

Location tells you:
→ where to go

Item scan tells you:
→ you picked correctly

---

## 3. The Flow (Simple)

```text id="pick_barcode_flow"
Go to Location → Scan Location → Pick Item → Scan Item → Confirm
```

---

## 4. Step-by-Step

---

### Step 1 — Open Picking in the App

* go to picking
* select the order

You will see:

→ item + exact location

---

### Step 2 — Go to the Location

Follow the system:

* zone
* row
* shelf
* bin

---

### Step 3 — Scan the Location Barcode

* scan the bin barcode

---

### What this does:

→ confirms you are in the **correct place**

---

### Step 4 — Pick the Item

* take the item from that bin

---

### Step 5 — Scan the Item

* scan the barcode

---

### What happens:

---

#### ✅ Correct Item

→ confirmed
→ move to next item

---

#### ⚠️ Not Matching / Issue

→ follow system guidance (Problem-Center)

---

## 5. What NOT To Do

Do NOT:

* pick without scanning location
* pick without scanning item
* search manually
* assume location is correct

---

If you skip location scan:

→ you might be in the wrong place

If you skip item scan:

→ you might pick the wrong item

---

## 6. Why This Matters

This step removes:

* searching
* hesitation
* human error

---

If done right:

→ picking becomes:

* fast
* repeatable
* accurate

---

## 7. What Happens in the System

Each pick creates:

→ **Location → Item → Order link**

---

This allows:

* full traceability
* error tracking
* performance insights

---

## 8. Common Mistakes

* scanning location but not item
* scanning item but not location
* picking from nearby bins
* rushing and skipping steps

---

## Rule

→ **Both scans are required. Always.**

---

## 9. What Good Looks Like

* go directly to bin
* scan location
* pick item
* scan item
* move on

---

No searching. No guessing.

---

```text id="pick_quick_rule"
Go →
Scan location →
Pick →
Scan item →
Confirm
```
