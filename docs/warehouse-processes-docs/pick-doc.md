# **Picking Orders (Find the Right Item Every Time)**

---

## 1. What Picking Means in LaSyncro

Picking is not just finding an item.

It is:
→ **selecting the exact correct item for an order—without hesitation**

---

Without LaSyncro:

* workers search shelves
* compare items manually
* rely on memory or labels

This leads to:

* wrong picks
* delays
* repeated checking

---

With LaSyncro:

→ The system tells you:

* what to pick
* where it is
* when it’s confirmed

---

## 2. What You Need Before You Start

* Orders ready for picking
* Items correctly stowed
* LaSyncro app open on your phone

---

## 3. The Picking Flow (Simple)

```text id="v3qk9x"
Locate → Pick → Confirm
```

No guessing. No searching.

---

## 4. Step-by-Step

---

### Step 1 — Open Picking on Your Phone

* Open LaSyncro
* Go to picking tasks

You will see:
→ the next item to pick

---

### Step 2 — Go to the Location

The system shows:
→ exactly where the item is stored

* shelf
* bin
* slot

---

### Step 3 — Identify the Item

At the location:

→ the system indicates the **exact item**

No need to:

* compare multiple options
* second-guess

---

### Step 4 — Pick the Item

* take the item
* prepare to confirm

---

### Step 5 — Confirm by Scanning

* scan the barcode using your phone

---

### What happens instantly:

---

#### ✅ Correct Item

→ confirmed
→ move to next item

---

#### ⚠️ Issue Detected (Problem-Center)

Examples:

* item damaged
* barcode not readable
* wrong item in location
* item missing

---

When this happens:

→ the issue is captured
→ logged in **Problem-Center**
→ routed for resolution

You do NOT:

* guess
* substitute
* delay the entire workflow
