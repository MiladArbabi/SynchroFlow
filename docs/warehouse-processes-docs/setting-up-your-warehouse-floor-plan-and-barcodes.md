# **Setting Up Your Warehouse (Floor Plan & Barcodes)**

---

## 1. What You Are Actually Doing

You are not just organizing shelves.

You are:

→ **turning your warehouse into a scannable system**

---

Every location becomes:

→ **a known, trackable, scannable point**

---

## 2. The Structure (Your Warehouse Blueprint)

Your warehouse must follow this hierarchy:

```text id="zone_hierarchy"
Warehouse → Zone → Row → Shelf → Bin
```

---

### What Each Level Means

* **Zone** → large section (e.g. A, B, C)
* **Row** → aisle within a zone
* **Shelf** → vertical level
* **Bin** → exact placement spot

---

## Key Principle

→ **Every bin is a unique location**

That is what the system uses.

---

## 3. Step 1 — Define Your Layout

Before printing anything:

---

### Divide your warehouse into Zones

* based on space or product type
* keep it simple (A, B, C…)

---

### Define Rows

* straight paths / aisles
* consistent direction

---

### Define Shelves

* vertical levels
* clearly separated

---

### Define Bins

* smallest unit
* where items actually sit

---

## Rule

→ If it can hold an item, it needs a bin

---

## 4. Step 2 — Generate Location Codes

Each bin must have a unique code.

---

### Recommended Format

```text id="location_code"
Z1-R2-S3-B4
```

---

### Meaning:

* Zone 1
* Row 2
* Shelf 3
* Bin 4

---

## Why This Matters

This code becomes:

→ the **identity of that physical space**

---

## 5. Step 3 — Generate Barcodes

Using LaSyncro:

* generate barcodes for each bin
* each barcode = one location

---

## Important

→ **Never reuse barcodes**

→ one barcode = one location forever

---

## 6. Step 4 — Place Barcodes Physically

This is where most setups fail.

---

### Placement Rules

* clearly visible
* easy to scan
* consistent position
* not blocked by items

---

### Recommended Placement

* front of shelf
* eye-level if possible
* same position across all shelves

---

## Rule

→ If it’s hard to scan, it will not be used

---

## 7. Step 5 — Validate Your Setup

Before using the system:

---

### Test each location

* scan the barcode
* confirm it matches expected location

---

### Walk the warehouse

Ask:

→ can I scan every bin easily?

---

If not:

→ fix it now

---

## 8. How This Changes Your Workflow

---

### Before

* “Put it anywhere”
* “Check that shelf”
* “It’s somewhere there”

---

### After

→ every item goes to:

* a specific bin
* a known location
* a scannable point

---

## 9. What Good Setup Looks Like

* every bin has a barcode
* all barcodes are scannable
* layout is consistent
* no “extra” storage spots

---

## 10. Common Mistakes to Avoid

* skipping bin-level barcodes
* placing barcodes inconsistently
* allowing unofficial storage areas
* not testing scans

---

If setup is weak:

→ your system will not be trusted

---

## 11. The Outcome

Once this is done:

→ stowing becomes guided
→ picking becomes instant
→ inventory becomes reliable

---

## Key Insight

→ **This setup eliminates “where is it?” from your warehouse**
