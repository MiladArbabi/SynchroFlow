# **Never Pick Without Scanning**

---

## 1. The Rule

→ **If it’s not scanned, it’s not correct.**

---

## 2. What This Means

Even if:

* the item looks right
* the packaging matches
* you are “sure” it’s correct

---

→ **It must still be scanned.**

---

## 3. Why This Rule Exists

In a warehouse:

* many items look similar
* small differences matter (size, version, SKU)
* visual checks are unreliable

---

Without scanning:

* wrong items get picked
* errors go unnoticed
* problems cannot be traced

---

## 4. What To Do (Always)

---

### Step 1 — Locate the Item

Go to the assigned location.

---

### Step 2 — Pick the Item

Take the item you believe is correct.

---

### Step 3 — Scan the Barcode

* use your phone
* scan before confirming

---

### Step 4 — Let the System Confirm

---

#### ✅ If Correct

→ proceed

---

#### ⚠️ If Not Correct

→ do NOT pick
→ follow system instructions

---

## 5. What NOT To Do

Do NOT:

* pick based on appearance
* skip scanning to save time
* assume correctness
* confirm without scanning

---

Even once.

Because:

→ one skipped scan = one untraceable mistake

---

## 6. What Happens If You Skip Scanning

* wrong item may be shipped
* issue cannot be tracked
* system data becomes unreliable

---

Over time:

→ trust in the system breaks

---

## 7. What Good Looks Like

* every item is scanned
* no assumptions are made
* confirmation comes from the system
* zero hesitation

---

## 8. Common Situations Where People Skip Scanning

* item looks identical
* barcode is hard to reach
* “I’ve picked this before”
* rushing to finish faster

---

These are exactly when mistakes happen.

---

## 9. How This Connects to Everything Else

Scanning ensures:

* picking accuracy
* correct packing
* fewer returns
* fewer refunds

---

## Key Insight

→ scanning is not a step
→ **it is the source of truth**

---

## 10. Quick Rule (Operator Shortcut)

```text id="5x7kpl"
If not scanned →
Not confirmed →
Do not pick
```
