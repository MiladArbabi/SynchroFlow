# **Item Found But Damaged**

---

## 1. What You’re Seeing

You locate the item.

But something is wrong:

* product is damaged
* packaging is torn or broken
* item is dirty or not sellable
* barcode is damaged or unreadable

---

## 2. What This Actually Means

This is a **SKU-Gap discovered during picking**.

---

The item:
→ exists in the system
→ but should NOT be used for fulfillment

---

This usually means:

* damage occurred during receiving or storage
* the issue was never logged
* the item was incorrectly stowed

---

## 3. What To Do (Immediately)

Follow this exactly:

---

### Step 1 — Do NOT Pick the Item

* do not use it
* do not assume it’s “good enough”

---

### Step 2 — Scan the Item

* use your phone to scan

This confirms:
→ whether it matches the order

---

### Step 3 — Log the Issue in Problem-Center

* select: **“Item Damaged”**
* confirm the issue

---

### Step 4 — Continue the Workflow

* move to the next item
* do not stop the order

---

## 4. What NOT To Do

Do NOT:

* pick the damaged item anyway
* try to fix it on the spot
* ignore minor damage
* replace it without logging

---

If you bypass the system:

→ bad inventory stays active
→ errors repeat
→ returns increase

---

## 5. What Happens Next (System Behavior)

Once logged:

* issue is recorded
* linked to:

  * SKU
  * location
  * order

---

Then:

→ converted into a **SKU-Gap (Job To Be Done)**

---

This ensures:

* item gets inspected
* fixed or removed
* prevented from future use

---

## 6. Why This Matters

If damaged items are picked:

* customers receive bad products
* returns increase
* refunds increase
* trust drops

---

If handled correctly:

→ damaged inventory is removed early

---

## 7. Root Cause (Why This Happens)

Common causes:

* damage during receiving
* poor storage conditions
* handling issues
* unreported problem-center

---

## Key Insight

→ this is not a picking problem
→ it is an **inventory quality problem**

---

## 8. What Good Looks Like

* damaged items are never picked
* issues are logged immediately
* no silent fixes
* system reflects real inventory condition

---

## 9. How To Prevent This

* inspect items during receiving
* log problem-center immediately
* avoid placing damaged items into storage
* maintain handling discipline

---
