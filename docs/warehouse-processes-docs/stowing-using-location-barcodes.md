# **Stowing Using Location Barcodes**

---

## 1. What This Means

Stowing is no longer:

→ “put the item somewhere”

It is:

→ **assigning an item to a specific, scannable location**

---

Every item must be:

→ **linked to a bin barcode**

---

## 2. The Rule

→ **No scan = no placement**

---

If a location is not scanned:

→ the item is not properly stored

---

## 3. The Flow (Simple)

```text id="stow_barcode_flow"
Scan Item → Scan Location → Confirm
```

---

## 4. Step-by-Step

---

### Step 1 — Open Stowing in the App

* go to stowing
* select or scan the item

---

### Step 2 — Scan the Item

* use your phone
* confirm item identity

---

### Step 3 — Go to the Assigned Location

The system shows:

→ where the item should go

---

### Step 4 — Scan the Location Barcode

* scan the bin barcode
* ensure it matches the intended location

---

### Step 5 — Confirm Placement

Once both scans are complete:

→ item is officially stored

---

## What Just Happened

You created a link:

→ **Item → Location → System**

---

## 5. What NOT To Do

Do NOT:

* place items without scanning location
* scan item only (and skip location)
* store items in “temporary spots”
* assume location without confirming

---

If you skip location scan:

→ the system loses track
→ picking will fail later

---

## 6. What Happens in the System

After confirmation:

* item is assigned to that bin
* location becomes searchable
* picking becomes precise

---

## 7. Why This Matters

This step determines:

* how fast picking will be
* whether items can be found
* how accurate inventory is

---

If done right:

→ no searching
→ no confusion
→ no delays

---

## 8. Common Mistakes

* scanning the wrong location
* placing item near (not in) the bin
* scanning once for multiple items
* skipping confirmation

---

## Rule

→ **Every item = its own scan + its own location confirmation**

---

## 9. What Good Looks Like

* item scanned
* location scanned
* exact placement
* no assumptions

---

## 10. Quick Action (Operator Shortcut)

```text id="stow_quick_rule"
Scan item →
Scan location →
Place →
Confirm
```
