# **Returns & Refunds (Handling What Comes Back)**

---

## 1. What Returns & Refunds Mean in LaSyncro

Shipping is not the end of the workflow.

Orders come back.

---

Returns are:
→ **feedback from reality**

---

Without a system:

* returns pile up
* items are checked manually
* decisions are inconsistent
* root causes are lost

---

With LaSyncro:

→ every return is:

* inspected
* recorded
* linked
* resolved

---

## 2. The Flow (Simple)

```text id="n8k2vd"
Receive Return → Inspect → Decide → Act → Record
```

---

## 3. Step-by-Step

---

### Step 1 — Receive the Returned Item

* returned package arrives
* open at return station

---

### What matters here:

→ this is not just unpacking
→ this is **re-entry into the system**

---

### Step 2 — Inspect the Item

At the inspection table:

Check for:

* product condition
* damage
* packaging state
* completeness

---

### Typical tools (real-world detail)

* lint / hair removal roller
* scissors
* basic inspection tools

👉 These are part of the process—not decoration

---

### Step 3 — Scan and Identify

* scan the item using your phone

---

### System links:

→ item
→ original order
→ history

---

### Step 4 — Decide the Outcome

Based on inspection:

---

#### ✅ Restock

* item is usable
* returned to inventory

---

#### ⚠️ Fix Required

* cleaning
* relabeling
* minor repair

→ becomes a **Job To Be Done**

---

#### ❌ Reject

* damaged beyond use
* removed from inventory

---

### Step 5 — Record the Outcome

Everything is logged:

* condition
* decision
* action taken

---

### Step 6 — Handle Refund / Correction

If needed:

* refund is issued
* order is corrected

---

Returns are not just cost.

They are:
→ **signals**

---

If ignored:

* refunds increase
* margins shrink

If managed:

* where errors happen

## 6. What Good Looks Like

* returns are processed immediately
* inspection is consistent
* decisions are clear
* inventory is updated correctly

## 7. Common Mistakes to Avoid

* restocking without inspection
* skipping system logging
* treating returns as separate from operations
* ignoring root cause

---

If you don’t track returns:

→ you lose the feedback loop
