## 0. Positioning (Anchor This First)

> *“What is actually happening with orders — economically, temporally, and operationally — based on what is truly known?”*

* Explain why
* Suggest what to do

## 2. FT2-Free: What Users Get (Truth-Scoped)

FT2-Free is **entitled observability**, not value extraction.

### 2.1 Allowed Surface (FT2-Free)

**From Layer 3 (FTEP):**

#### Context

* Period (from → to)
* Orders observed (nullable)

#### Totals (raw)

* Revenue total (nullable)
* Cost total (nullable)
* Currency

#### Outcome (downgraded)

* `positive | negative | unknown`
* No percentages
* No thresholds exposed

#### Trend (coarse)

* `up | down | flat | unknown`
* No deltas
* No explanation

#### Data Coverage

* `% completeness` (nullable)
* Signals *how much is missing*, not why

---

### 2.2 What FT2-Free Explicitly Withholds

| Category         | Reason                       |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Margin %         | Intelligence leakage         |
| Cost breakdown   | Requires Products / Finances |
| Loss drivers     | Causation                    |
| Comparisons      | Analytics-owned              |
| Recommendations  | Not FT2                      |
| Historical depth | Paid deepening               |

FT2-Free must make the user feel:

> “The system clearly knows more than it’s showing.”
