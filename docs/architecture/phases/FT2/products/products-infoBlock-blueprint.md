# 📘 Products Module — InfoBlock Blueprint (North Star)

## Global Principles (Apply to ALL InfoBlocks)

These rules are non-negotiable:

1. **One infoBlock = one primary question**
2. **Rows represent system truths, not interpretations**
3. **Numbers must be explainable without tooltips**
4. **Action emerges from structure, not instructions**
5. **No duplicate semantics across blocks**

If two rows answer the same SMB question → one of them is wrong.

---

## 1️⃣ InfoBlock #1 — **Products Overview**

### Purpose

> Establish the shape of the product catalog at a glance.

This block answers:

> “What products do I currently have?”

### This block is **existence-based**, not performance-based

---

### Rows (Authoritative)

| Row                        | System Truth                                | SMB Question Answered        |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| **Active Products**        | Products currently available for sale       | “What can I sell right now?” |
| **Product Variations**     | Total sellable options across products      | “How broad is my offering?”  |
| **Products Not Available** | Products that exist but cannot be sold      | “What’s not sellable?”       |
| **New Products Added**     | Products created within the FT2 date window | “Did I add anything new?”    |

---

### Footer / Status Line (Meta, not data)

> **PRODUCT EXISTENCE SHOWN**
> **SALES AND VALUE DETAILED ELSEWHERE**

---

## 2️⃣ InfoBlock #2 — **Product Sales Reality**

### Purpose

> Establish whether products are actually selling.

This block answers:

> “Are my products generating sales at all?”

---

### Rows (Non-Negotiable)

| Row                               | System Truth                            | SMB Question Answered                      |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Products Sold**                 | Products included in at least one order | “What products are selling?”               |
| **Products With No Sales**        | Products not included in any order      | “What products didn’t sell?”               |
| **Share of Catalog Contributing** | % of products that generated any sales  | “How much of my catalog actually matters?” |

---

### Hard Rules

* No revenue amounts here
* No rankings or product identification
* This block shows **presence of sales**, not success

---

## 3️⃣ InfoBlock #3 — **Revenue Dependence**

### Purpose

> Expose *structural dependence*, not performance.

This block answers:

> “How dependent is my revenue on a small part of my catalog?”

---

### Rows (Structure-Focused)

| Row                                 | System Truth                                    | SMB Question Answered                    |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Revenue Concentration Level**     | Degree to which revenue depends on few products | “Am I dependent on just a few products?” |
| **Revenue From Remaining Products** | Sales value outside the main revenue drivers    | “How much comes from everything else?”   |

---

### Rules

* No product counts required
* No trend interpretation
* This block **never blocks FT2**, it informs it

---

## 4️⃣ InfoBlock #4 — **Pricing & Discounts**

### Purpose

> Surface discount exposure without interpretation.

This block answers:

> “How much of my sales rely on discounts?”

---

### Rows (Visibility Only)

| Row                              | System Truth                           | SMB Question Answered                     |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Products Sold With Discounts** | Products sold below regular price      | “How many products were discounted?”      |
| **Share of Sales Discounted**    | % of sales value affected by discounts | “How much of my sales rely on discounts?” |

---

### Hard Rules

* No margins
* No profitability framing
* No judgment or optimization language

---

## 5️⃣ InfoBlock #5 — **Product Value Flow** 💎

### Purpose

> Reveal *where product value sits relative to orders*.

This block answers:

> “Where is my product value right now?”

This is the **order-aligned value visibility block**.

---

### Rows (Order-Relative)

| Row                                   | System Truth                         | SMB Question Answered                 |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| **Products With Sales Activity**      | Products tied to any sales activity  | “What’s currently involved in sales?” |
| **Products With Pending Orders**      | Products in orders not yet completed | “What’s still in progress?”           |
| **Products Tied to Completed Orders** | Products in completed orders         | “What’s already done?”                |
| **Products With No Active Orders**    | Products not tied to any order       | “What’s currently inactive?”          |

> Same wording with or without WMS-lite.
> WMS-lite deepens accuracy but never changes semantics.

---

### Hard Rules

* No warehouse language in free tier
* No execution diagnosis
* This is **value position**, not outcome

---

## 6️⃣ InfoBlock #6 — **Product Flow (CPT)** 💎

### Purpose

> Expose *time pressure* on product value.

This block answers:

> “How much product value is under time pressure?”

This is the **time-based operational visibility block**.

---

### Rows (Time-Aligned)

| Row                              | System Truth                              | SMB Question Answered          |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Products Within Cutoff**       | Products progressing within expected time | “What’s comfortably on track?” |
| **Products Approaching Cutoff**  | Products nearing time limits              | “What needs attention soon?”   |
| **Products Past Cutoff**         | Products beyond expected time             | “What’s already late?”         |
| **Products With Unknown Timing** | Products lacking reliable timing signals  | “What can’t I time yet?”       |

---

### Hard Rules

* No causes shown
* No blame or urgency language
* This is **pressure visibility**, not delay explanation
