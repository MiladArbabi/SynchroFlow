## 1️⃣ `LOADING`

### Title

**Analyzing session data…**

### Message

We’re checking whether customer session data is available for this store.

### CTA

❌ None

* No implication that data *will* exist
* No mention of tracking, setup, or failure
* Purely transitional

---

## 2️⃣ `NO_SESSIONS`

### Title

**No customer sessions detected**

### Message

No customer sessions have been recorded for this store yet. Without session data, behavioral signals cannot be computed.

### CTA

**Set up session tracking**

→ Focus task: `connect-tracking`

### Why this is correct

* States absence, not cause
* Explicitly says *what cannot be computed*, not *what should happen*
* CTA is infrastructural, not corrective

---

## 3️⃣ `INSUFFICIENT_SIGNAL`

### Title

**Insufficient session data**

### Message

Customer sessions are being recorded, but there isn’t enough data yet to reliably derive behavioral signals.

### CTA

**Verify data collection**

→ Focus task: `verify-events`

### Why this is correct

* “Insufficient” is quantitative, not judgmental
* “Reliably” signals safety without promising future readiness
* No suggestion that “more traffic” or “waiting” will solve it

---

## 4️⃣ `SIGNAL_READY`

### Title

**Session data is available**

### Message

Sufficient session data has been recorded to compute behavioral signals for this store.

### CTA

❌ None

### Why this is correct

* Does *not* say insights exist
* Does *not* say anything will be shown
* Establishes readiness only, nothing more
