**Status**: Planned / Upcoming (Phase 6)

## Purpose & Vision: The Cornerstone of Customer Understanding

The Customer 360 view is engineered to be the **absolute single source of truth for every customer interaction and data point** within SynchroFlow. Its mission is to eradicate the operational drag caused by fragmented customer information typically siloed across sales channels (Shopify, Amazon), support desks (Zendesk, email), and fulfillment systems. This unification is achieved by SynchroFlow's Integration Service, consolidating data into a central profile.

By presenting this unified data intelligently, Customer 360 empowers your teams—from frontline support to strategic marketing—to:

* **Deliver Hyper-Personalized Experiences**: Understand the full customer context immediately.
* **Increase Operational Efficiency**: Eliminate time wasted hunting for information across multiple tools.
* **Boost Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Identify and nurture high-value customers proactively.
* **Reduce Churn**: Address issues faster and with greater insight.

## Anticipated Layout & Information Architecture

Accessed via `/customers/:id` (and embedded contextually in **Echo Hub**), the Customer 360 view will likely utilize a **multi-section layout optimized for scannability and quick access to key information**:

1.  **Header Section**: Prominent display of customer name, primary contact info (email/phone), key tags (e.g., "VIP", "High Return Rate"), and potentially the account creation date/source. _Visual Goal_: Immediate identification and high-level segmentation.
2.  **Key Metrics Card/Row**: A visually distinct area showcasing critical value indicators like **LTV**, **AOV**, **Total Orders**, **Margin**, **Refund Rate**, and **Last Order Date**. _Visual Goal_: Rapid assessment of customer value and recency. [Image placeholder: Customer KPIs card]
3.  **Main Content Area (Potentially Tabbed or Sectioned)**:
    * **Order History**: A searchable, sortable `DataGrid` linking directly to **Order 360**.
    * **Communication History**: A feed/list of interactions from **Echo Hub**, linking to specific conversations.
    * **Profile Details**: Comprehensive contact info, address management, notes, and custom fields.
    * **Activity Timeline (Future)**: Chronological event log.
    _Visual Goal_: Allow users to quickly drill down into specific areas of interest without excessive scrolling.

## Core Functionality & Data Presentation (Planned)

Customer 360 intelligently aggregates and presents:

1.  **Profile & Identification**:
    * Core contact details.
    * Comprehensive address management (Shipping/Billing) with editing capabilities.
    * Key metadata & Customer Tags/Segments (viewing and potentially editing).

2.  **Key Metrics At-a-Glance**:
    * Calculated LTV, AOV, Total Orders, Profitability Insights, Recency.

3.  **Order History**:
    * Searchable, sortable, filterable `DataGrid` of past orders.
    * Direct links to **Order 360**.

4.  **Communication & Support History (Echo Hub Integration)**:
    * Chronological feed/list of tickets, chats, emails.
    * Direct links to **Echo Hub** conversations.
    * Customer Sentiment Score (AI-derived, Future).

5.  **Direct Actions (Planned)**:
    * Edit customer contact details/addresses.
    * Add/remove customer tags.
    * Add internal notes to the customer profile.
    * Potentially initiate contact (e.g., trigger an email via integration).

6.  **Activity Timeline (Future)**:
    * Granular event log.

## The Central Hub: Integration with SynchroFlow

Customer 360 acts as a nexus, both consuming and providing data across the platform:

* **Echo Hub**: Customer 360 context is **embedded directly** in the agent view. Support history displayed originates from Echo Hub. [Link to Echo Hub Documentation]
* **Order 360**: Customer snapshots displayed on Order 360 originate here. Order history links navigate directly *to* Order 360 views. [Link to Order 360 Documentation]
* **FinOps Command Center**: Aggregated customer metrics roll up into high-level business reporting.
* **WMS (Indirectly)**: Shipping addresses originate from customer profiles.
* **Marketing/Segmentation (Future)**: Provides the rich dataset needed for targeting.
