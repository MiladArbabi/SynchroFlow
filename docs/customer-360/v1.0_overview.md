# Customer 360 👤🔄

**Status**: Planned / Upcoming (Phase 6)

---

## Purpose & Vision: The Cornerstone of Customer Understanding

The Customer 360 view is engineered to be the **absolute single source of truth for every customer interaction and data point** within SynchroFlow. Its mission is to eradicate the operational drag caused by fragmented customer information typically siloed across sales channels (Shopify, Amazon), support desks (Zendesk, email), and fulfillment systems.

By consolidating this disparate data into one unified, instantly accessible profile, Customer 360 empowers your teams—from frontline support to strategic marketing—to:

* **Deliver Hyper-Personalized Experiences**: Understand the full customer context immediately.
* **Increase Operational Efficiency**: Eliminate time wasted hunting for information across multiple tools.
* **Boost Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Identify and nurture high-value customers proactively.
* **Reduce Churn**: Address issues faster and with greater insight.

---

## Core Functionality & Data Presentation (Planned)

Accessed via `/customers/:id` (or embedded within views like Echo Hub), Customer 360 will intelligently aggregate and present:

1.  **Profile & Identification**:
    * Core contact details (Name, Email, Phone).
    * Comprehensive address management (Shipping/Billing).
    * Key metadata: Account origin, creation date, Customer Tags/Segments.
    * _Visual Goal_: A clean header section providing immediate identification.

2.  **Key Metrics At-a-Glance**:
    * **Lifetime Value (LTV)**, **Average Order Value (AOV)**, **Total Orders**.
    * **Profitability Insights**: Overall customer margin, Refund Rate/Value.
    * **Recency**: Last Order Date.
    * _Visual Goal_: A prominent card or dashboard-like section displaying these critical value indicators, potentially with trend sparklines. [Image placeholder: Customer KPIs card]

3.  **Order History**:
    * A **searchable, sortable, and filterable** data grid of all past orders across channels.
    * Displays Order ID (deep link to **Order 360**), Date, Status, Total Value per order.
    * _Visual Goal_: Efficient table allowing quick lookups and analysis of purchasing behavior.

4.  **Communication & Support History (Echo Hub Integration)**:
    * A chronological feed of all associated support tickets, chats, and emails from **Echo Hub**.
    * Includes Ticket ID (deep link to **Echo Hub** conversation), Subject, Date, Status, Agent.
    * **Customer Sentiment Score**: AI-derived indicator based on communication analysis (Future).
    * _Visual Goal_: A scrollable timeline providing context on past interactions and sentiment.

5.  **Activity Timeline (Future)**:
    * Granular log of events: Placed Order, Opened Ticket, Received Shipment, Site Visits, Abandoned Carts, etc.

## The Central Hub: Integration with SynchroFlow

Customer 360 acts as a nexus, both consuming and providing data across the platform:

* **Echo Hub**: Customer 360 context is **embedded directly** in the agent view, providing instant background for efficient support. Support history displayed originates from Echo Hub. [Link to Echo Hub Documentation]
* **Order 360**: Customer snapshots displayed on Order 360 originate here. Order history links navigate directly *to* Order 360 views. [Link to Order 360 Documentation]
* **FinOps Command Center**: Aggregated customer metrics (LTV, AOV) roll up into high-level business reporting.
* **WMS (Indirectly)**: Shipping addresses used in fulfillment originate from customer profiles.
* **Marketing/Segmentation (Future)**: Provides the rich dataset needed for effective targeting.

    * **Problem Solved**: Eliminates asking repetitive questions ("What's your order number? Have you contacted us before?"). Provides full context instantly.

    * **Problem Solved**: Consolidates order history and communication logs for managing B2B relationships.
    * **Benefit**: Better client understanding, identification of upsell opportunities.

    * **Problem Solved**: Creates a unified dataset for segmentation and analysis, replacing manual data merging.
    * **Benefit**: More effective targeting, better campaign ROI.
* **Management**:
    * **Problem Solved**: Provides insights into customer value, retention, and behavior trends across the entire business.
