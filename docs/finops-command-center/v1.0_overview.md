# FinOps Command Center 📊💰

**Status**: Foundation Implemented, Core Widgets Integrated

---

## Purpose & Vision

The FinOps Command Center serves as the **unified dashboard for finance and operations**, breaking down traditional silos between these critical business functions. Its primary goal is to provide a real-time, holistic view of your e-commerce business's financial health *in direct relation* to its operational performance.

Instead of switching between accounting software, inventory management systems, and BI tools, the FinOps Command Center aggregates key data points into a single, actionable view, enabling faster decision-making and highlighting the direct impact of operational efficiency on financial outcomes.

---

## Core Functionality & Insights

This dashboard allows users to:

* **Monitor Key Financial KPIs**: Track top-line revenue, gross margin, and overall profitability trends.
* **Assess Operational Health**: Understand inventory value, stock levels, and potential risks like stockouts.
* **Visualize Cash Flow**: Analyze historical cash movements and (in future versions) simulate potential scenarios.
* **Quantify Efficiency Gains**: Directly observe the operational expenditure (Opex) saved through SynchroFlow's automation via the A-Opex Gauge.
* **Identify Correlations**: See how inventory levels impact sales, how fulfillment costs affect margins, and how operational bottlenecks influence cash flow.

---

## Key Widgets & Components

The FinOps Command Center is built upon the customizable dashboard layout and features several core widgets:

* **KPI Cards (`KpiCard`)**: Display high-level, critical metrics with trend indicators. Examples include:
    * `Gross Revenue`
    * `Gross Margin`
    * `Inventory Value`
* **A-Opex Gauge (`AOpexGauge`)**: The flagship widget quantifying the estimated labor cost savings achieved through SynchroFlow's automated actions across different modules (Echo Hub, WMS, etc.). This directly demonstrates ROI. 
* **Cash Flow Widget (`CashFlowWidget`)**: A chart (using `ReactApexChart`) visualizing inflows and outflows over time. *Future versions will incorporate predictive forecasting and simulation capabilities.*
* **Inventory Health Widget (`InventoryHealthWidget`)**: A summary table or visualization highlighting SKUs that are healthy, at risk of stocking out, or currently stocked out, often including days of stock remaining.

*(Note: Widgets displayed depend on user customization and potentially their subscription plan)*.

## Data Sources (High-Level)

The FinOps Command Center achieves its unified view by integrating data pulled from various sources via the SynchroFlow platform, including:

* Sales channels (e.g., Shopify, Amazon) for revenue and order data.
* Inventory Truth (SynchroFlow's C++ Core & Database) for real-time stock levels.
* Cost data (Product Costs, Shipping Rates) entered or integrated into SynchroFlow.
* Operational data from other modules (e.g., WMS pick/pack times, Echo Hub ticket volume - contributing to A-Opex).
