# LaSyncro WMS-Lite ("The Digital Twin" Module) 📦✨

**Status**: Planned / Upcoming (Phase 5)

---

## Purpose & Vision

The LaSyncro WMS-Lite ("The Digital Twin") is the core of the "$349 Spark" plan. Its entire mission is to **transform a brand owner's chaotic, messy storage room into a professional, error-proof, high-efficiency fulfillment center.**

It achieves this by creating a 1-to-1 **"Digital Twin"** of the physical inventory. The core philosophy is simple: it links two things: a **Product Barcode (what it is)** to a **Location Barcode (where it is)**.

The goal is *not* to compete with complex, enterprise WMS, but to provide the essential, error-proof system that replaces a high-cost "chaos manager" with a low-cost "automation system."

---

## The 5 Core Workflows

This system is built around five simple, discrete modules that mirror a professional fulfillment operation.

### Module 1: SETUP (The One-Time Map)
* **Analogy:** Giving "Street Addresses" to the shelves.
* **Flow:** The brand owner uses the app to create and print unique barcode labels for all physical locations (e.g., `LOC-A01`, `LOC-A02`). These stickers are placed on the shelves, turning the "messy room" into a "mapped grid."

### Module 2: RECEIVE (The "Name Tag")
* **Analogy:** Giving "Name Tags" to all new products.
* **Flow:** When a supplier shipment arrives, the VA scans the Purchase Order (PO) on the box. The app confirms the contents (e.g., "100 x Blue Shirt - M") and then uses the Print Station to print 100 identical "Item Barcodes" (e.g., `PROD-12345`). Each item is tagged.

### Module 3: STOW (The "Phonebook")
* **Analogy:** Creating the phonebook entry ("Product X lives at Address Y").
* **Flow:** The VA scans the location (e.g., `LOC-A01`), scans the item (`PROD-12345`), and enters the quantity ("100"). This action writes to the `Inventory_Ledger` and creates the core "Digital Twin" link.

### Module 4: PICK (The "Google Maps")
* **Analogy:** The optimized "Google Maps" route for picking orders.
* **Flow:** The VA starts a "Batch" of orders. The app provides an optimized path, directing them to the first location.
    1.  **Scan Location:** VA scans `LOC-A01`. (App verifies it's the right place).
    2.  **Scan Item:** VA picks the item and scans `PROD-12345`. (App verifies it's the right product with a `BEEP!`).
    3.  A scan of the wrong item or location results in a loud `BZZT!` (Error), eliminating mis-picks.

### Module 5: PACK & SHIP (The "Final Check")
* **Analogy:** The "Checkout Counter" and "Label Printer."
* **Flow:** At the packing station, the VA scans the picked items one last time. The app auto-pulls the order, confirms it's 100% accurate, and then instantly prints the correct UPS/FedEx shipping label via the Print Station.

## Architecture & Hardware

* **Frontend (The App):** A **React Native (iOS/Android) app** is the only tool the warehouse VA uses. It leverages the device camera for scanning.
* **Backend (The Brain):** A unified **Node.js/JavaScript** backend. It serves as the API to orchestrate requests and as the core **"Atomic Inventory" Engine**, handling all critical database writes (Stow, Pick) with database-level transactions to prevent race conditions.
* **Printing (The "Robust" Link):** We will use the **LaSyncro Print Station**—a tiny desktop app (or Raspberry Pi app) connected to a standard thermal printer. This bypasses unreliable Bluetooth/Wi-Fi drivers, ensuring robust, high-speed printing of item, location, and shipping labels.

## Integration with SynchroFlow

This is where the WMS-Lite derives its full power:

* **Inventory Accuracy**: All actions directly read from and write to the central **Node.js Inventory Core (Atomic Inventory Truth)**, ensuring data consistency everywhere.
* **Order 360 Visibility**: The real-time status updates (e.g., "Picked," "Packed") are instantly visible on the **Order 360** page, allowing customer service to see exactly where an order is in the fulfillment process.
* **Ops-Intel Engine**: Data points like pick times, pack times, and accuracy rates feed directly into the **Ops-Intel Engine** to quantify labor costs and efficiency gains, contributing to the **A-Opex Gauge**.

## Future Enhancements (Post-Launch)

### Module 6: MANAGE (The "Auditor")
* **Cycle Counts:** A mobile workflow to scan a location and have the app prompt the VA to count its contents, verifying the "Digital Twin" is accurate.
* **Inventory Moves:** A simple tool to move stock from one location to another (Scan `LOC-A`, Scan Item, Scan `LOC-B`).
* **Adjustments:** A flow to scan an item and mark it as "Damaged" or "Missing," removing it from sellable inventory.

---

## Key Differentiators

* **True Digital Twin:** Simplifies inventory by linking *what* (Product) to *where* (Location).
* **Error-Proof Workflow:** Scan-verification at every critical step (Pick, Pack) eliminates human error.
* **Robust Printing:** The Print Station model is a dedicated hardware solution that solves the #1 failure point of mobile warehouse tech.
* **Atomic Accuracy:** Built upon the **Node.js "Atomic Inventory" core** (using database transactions) to prevent overselling and race conditions.
