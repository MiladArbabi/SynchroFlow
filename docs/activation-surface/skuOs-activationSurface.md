## Core Insight (Locked)

This ActivationSurface does not sell inventory intelligence.

It removes the illusion that replenishment decisions are currently safe.

When this surface works, the merchant does not ask:

> “Should I activate?”

They realize:

> **“I am already making these decisions blind.”**

At that point, activation is no longer optional.
It is **operational hygiene**.
