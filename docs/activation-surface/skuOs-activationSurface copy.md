## 1️⃣ Identity + Blind Spot

**(Doctrine: One Dominant Pain · No Neutral Inaction)**

> **“Right now, you don’t know which products will stock out first.”**

**Why this is correct**

* One decision only: *replenishment priority*
* No growth framing
* No future promise
* This is objectively true pre-integration

## Why This Works

SKU-OS activation doesn’t sell *inventory intelligence*.
It removes the illusion that replenishment decisions are currently safe.

The merchant realizes:

> *“I am already exposed to stockouts.
> I’m just blind to where.”*

At that point, activation isn’t a feature decision.
It’s **risk control**.
