The problem

Five fracture lines in every SMB operation

You have Shopify, a warehouse tool, spreadsheets, and WhatsApp. None of them talk to each other. The result isn't inefficiency — it's blindness.

01
Data fragmentation
Revenue lives in Shopify. Stock in a WMS. Costs in accounting. No single view of your true net cash position — so you build one in a spreadsheet that's already stale.
02
Data silos
Your WMS doesn't know your customer LTV. Your accounting tool doesn't know which orders are blocked. The signal that matters exists nowhere.
03
Spreadsheet chaos
Every SMB has a master spreadsheet. Usually several. Trusted by no one except the person who built them — who no longer remembers exactly why.
04
Firefighting culture
You find out about a stockout when a customer complains. You discover a margin problem in the quarterly report. You learn about an SLA breach from a 1-star review.
05
Coordination gap
Three to five warehouse staff. WhatsApp threads. Sticky notes. No system of record for who picked what, what arrived, or what shipped today.

The solution

Eight intelligence domains.
One sovereign source of truth.

LaSyncro ingests your Shopify data via OAuth and runs it through an immutable event-sourced pipeline. Every module draws from the same ledger — so the data is always consistent, always live.

Fulfillment queue
Replaces WhatsApp coordination
Constraint-aware, SLA-ranked pick surface. Know exactly what ships today and why.
Inventory & demand
Replaces stock spreadsheet
Velocity, days-of-stock, reorder signals. No more discovering stockouts from customers.
Finances & margin
Replaces monthly accountant report
Per-order gross margin, real-time. Know your true profitability before month-end.
Cash flow
Replaces Excel cash flow model
30/60/90-day position. Blocked revenue. Know if payroll is safe before you need to.
Returns
Replaces manual reconciliation
Return rate, leakage, restock tracking. Catch the patterns before they become margin problems.
Customer LTV
Replaces CRM guesswork
RFM model, churn risk, tier derivation. Know which customers to protect before they leave.
Alerts
Replaces reactive panic
Proactive constraint and SLA signals. Surface issues before they become customer complaints.
WMS-lite
Replaces WhatsApp + sticky notes
Full pick → pack → ship pipeline, ledger-backed. A solo operator with a smartphone can run it.

SMB merchants don't need enterprise software. They need the operational clarity that enterprise software promises — at a price point and complexity level that fits a 5-person team running a $2M Shopify store.

The data already exists. Shopify has it all. What's missing is the pipeline that transforms raw commerce events into operational decisions. LaSyncro is that pipeline.

Why we built this

I was the merchant
living in the spreadsheets.

For three years, my mornings looked the same. 7:03am. Four tabs open before I'd had coffee. Shopify for the orders. A warehouse spreadsheet nobody else understood. A WhatsApp thread with 47 unread messages. And the master file: cashflow_v3_FINAL_miladREVISED.xlsx. 79 tabs. 23 of them broken.

I'd spend the first two hours of every day not running the business — reconstructing it. Answering the same six survival questions manually, with stale data, every single morning. I missed hikes. I missed the gym. I missed my friends' football games. Not because the business was doing badly. Because the operations were invisible, and invisible operations demand constant human attention to keep from collapsing.

One Tuesday at 8:47pm, still at my desk reconciling a return that didn't match the inventory that didn't match the Shopify order, I looked up and thought: the data exists. The pipeline doesn't. So I built it. The first thing I did when it worked? I went for a hike. At 10am on a Wednesday. Because I finally could.
